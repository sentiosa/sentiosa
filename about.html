Hi, I’m Božica

I’m a photographer, videographer and visual storyteller from Montenegro, with a deep love for cinematography and film.

I travel often and love taking my work wherever life takes me — so you can book me anywhere in the world.

Two years ago I opened my own creative company, where I get to explore different ideas and projects, from photography and video to creating my own board game.

When I’m not behind the camera, you’ll probably find me practicing yoga, meditating, travelling, or looking for my next source of inspiration. For me it’s not only about the final image, but about the connection and experience we create along the way — I want working together to feel relaxed, natural and genuinely enjoyable.

If you’d like to know a little more about the person behind the camera, you can find me on my personal Instagram or follow along on YouTube, where I share bits and pieces from my travels and life along the way.
